Prologue · Robert Fader

Before you begin

This is not just another English textbook. It is an invitation to approach the language in a different way: not only to learn words and rules, but also to discover the stories, people, and contexts that give them meaning. The aim of this book is not to praise English culture as a whole. Rather, it is an attempt to understand the English-speaking world, whose influence and cultural prominence have a global reach. Learning a language also allows us to look at that world with curiosity, ask questions, and develop our own perspective on it.

Outsiders is intended for those who want to learn English in a new way. Throughout its pages, language study is at the heart of the story: the dialogues, situations, and characters accompany the learning process and give it continuity. Some of these characters come from my novels. Luis, Natalia, Lucy, Mark, Alicia, and others are part of this journey. Through their encounters, conversations, and stories, I hope that English will stop feeling like a collection of isolated exercises and become a more engaging and enjoyable experience.

My hope is that this textbook will awaken students’ curiosity, that they will find it appealing, and that they will enjoy their classes. Learning a language can also be a way of discovering other perspectives and enjoying a good story. I hope you enjoy this journey.

Robert Fader
